## ⭐ Flagged for Frank

1. **Decision models are now a three-way commodity race: Jev (hosted), OpenAI Decisions API (hosted, closed), and open weights from Cloudflare and AWS, all inside about 10 days.** Clef-flash at 38.8 ms and Decider at ~115 ms on a consumer GPU mean a per-action guardrail check is cheap enough to run on every agent step, not just on flagged ones. **Second-order: with four interchangeable options behind a shared Jev-style interface, the scarce asset is your own labelled decision data (which actions were allowed, blocked, escalated), not the model, so start capturing that trace now.** Clef's RL fine-tuning service explicitly sells that loop via AI Gateway data capture.

2. **Treat vendor benchmarks as marketing until run on your data.** The one independent test found Clef-flash agreeing with Jev 83% of the time but losing on triage, and Decider's own card admits 50.5% on Hard items. **Second-order: oversight models fail where the decision is ambiguous, which is exactly where a gaming-compliance escalation matters, so a tiered design (fast scorer on everything, reasoning or human review on low-confidence calls) is the defensible architecture.** Decider's calibration figure (ECE 0.052) is the metric to look for, since confidence thresholds drive the routing.

3. **The agent-containment story keeps escalating, and it is turning into a notification and audit duty.** OpenAI notifying 100+ organisations, the HF breach, Green's agent-worm framing, and AI-found bugs being exploited in 4 days all point the same way. **Second-order: expect regulators and your own security team to ask for proof of an agent's capability boundary and shutdown path, which lines up with the kill-switch bills and MGA's continuous-monitoring language; a decision model sitting between the agent and its tools is both the control and the evidence log.** Also check that nothing in your model tooling auto-enables remote code (the Unsloth lesson).

4. **Hackathon-buildable and on-device angle.** Decider 2B runs at ~153 ms on a MacBook and Clef-flash on a used RTX 3090, so a local personalisation-guardrail demo (score each recommended offer against responsible-gambling rules, with calibrated confidence) is a weekend build. **Second-order: on-device decisions keep player data in-jurisdiction, which is a selling point under EU AI Act and GDPR automated-decision scrutiny (the Dutch DPA's reported €825M Uber fine for automated decisions without human oversight is the cautionary case, date not verified here).** Context Language Models is worth a read for cheaper long-running agent monitoring.
