## Research & Papers

- **OpenAI's internal Astra solved ten open problems** in maths and theoretical CS, with formal Lean proofs published to GitHub — including a non-sofic group construction and new sphere-packing bounds. The Lean formalisation is what makes this checkable rather than a press release. [Decoding Data Science](https://decodingdatascience.com/openai-august2026/)
- **Andrej Karpathy is on Anthropic's pretraining team** — still the clearest signal on where the scale-vs-architecture argument settles. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrej_Karpathy)
- **Yann LeCun's Advanced Machine Intelligence Labs** continues as the counterweight to pure-scale orthodoxy. Useful framing when justifying architecture choices internally instead of defaulting to the largest model. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yann_LeCun)
- **Reasoning research themes trending on arXiv**: risk control for reasoning under a compute budget, test-time compute scaling via multi-agent synergy (TMAS), and skepticism papers on whether auto-research systems actually reason scientifically. 🎯 The compute-budget-vs-reasoning-quality tradeoff is the practical one for production agents. [TMAS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.10344) · [Workflow Closure Is Not Scientific Closure](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.26200)
- **Deontic reasoning benchmarks are appearing** (DeonticBench, DAR: Deontic Reasoning with Agentic Harnesses) — benchmarking whether agents can reason over *rules and obligations*. 🎯 That is the exact shape of responsible-gaming and licensing-condition logic. Worth a proper read. [DeonticBench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04443) · [DAR](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05009)
- **DARPA flew an F-16 fully under AI control** in a real-world test — a milestone for autonomy in high-consequence, hard-real-time environments. [Buildfast](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/collection/ai-industry-news-trends)
