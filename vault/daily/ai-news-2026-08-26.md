## Flagged for Frank

**Agent payments & accountability (#4) — highest relevance.** AP2 + NIST agent identity + AI AGENT Act (S.5051) all point the same way: any agent that can commit funds needs a signed, short-lived, task-bounded authorisation that travels with the request, plus tamper-evident logs. For anything in the Bally's stack where an agent touches a wallet, bonus, or entitlement, this is the design pattern to adopt now rather than retrofit. Pair it with Cloudflare WriteGuard (yesterday's briefing) for write-scope limits.

**Google Cloud's agent governance playbook (#5)** maps almost one-to-one onto the prototyping-platform governance questions in the RFC — task-level provenance, dynamic permissions, automatic escalation. Cheap to lift as a checklist for promotion gates.

**SpaceX/Grok in production support (#14)** is the proof point worth citing internally: conversational agents handling thousands of real customer interactions in a high-volume, low-tolerance service. Directly analogous to player support, and stronger evidence than any vendor deck.

**Mac mini M6 at $899 (#10)** — a credible local-inference box for hackathon kits and offline prototyping. Two or three of these are cheaper than sustained cloud inference for a weekend build, and they remove the API-key/data-egress conversation entirely.

**Compliance adjacency:** EU AI Act Art. 50 disclosure obligations (live since 2 Aug) plus the AI-content provenance trend — ARIA is now excluding fully AI-generated tracks from its charts — mean player-facing bots and any AI-generated marketing content need machine-readable labelling and a "you are talking to a machine" disclosure. Worth confirming who owns that in the current compliance map.

**Recommendation systems:** no new frontier research in the last 24h. The relevant thread remains real-time decisioning tied to consent state and responsible-gambling signals rather than static curation — flagged here mainly because #4 and #5 are the governance layer that personalisation work will eventually have to sit inside.

**Watch tomorrow:** Nvidia's earnings call tone on vendor financing (#1) is the best available read on whether AI infrastructure budgets tighten in 2027 — which would show up in internal platform spend before it shows up anywhere else.
