## Research & Papers

- ⭐ **Harness-Zero distills harness behaviour into the weights — and then beats the harness it learned from.** Peking University, 21 Sep. An "agent-as-harness" training loop that folds behaviours from optimised coding/agent harnesses back into a student model. On SpreadsheetBench Verified, AppWorld and USPTO Retrosynthesis, **removing the harness after distillation lifts the base model from 23.3% to 44.3% macro-average — against 41.7% with the specialised harness still attached.** Agent-as-harness beats code-as-harness distillation 81.1% vs 78.1% across six settings, recovering 82.3% of harness-induced behaviour. **Third independent result in two weeks pointing the same way: harness engineering is a temporary tax, not a moat.** [HF paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.24974)
- ⭐ **RRSI regularises recursive self-improvement of agent harnesses and cuts policy tokens 30%.** Posted 22 Sep. Adds **temporal-annealed bundle limits plus a critic/pruner** to filter benchmark-specific proposals. Across eight benchmarks: **up to +14.1 points on training tasks, up to +4.7 on five out-of-distribution tests, and 30% fewer policy tokens** than unregularised evolution. The argument is that regularisation biases self-improvement toward generalisable mechanisms rather than memorised task patterns — i.e. it's an overfitting control for self-improving agents. [HF paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.24972)
- ⭐ **Tencent ARC Lab published GameHorizon: 5,000 hours of AAA gameplay across 21 games, 47 models, 1M+ evaluation invocations.** Released 21 Sep as three pieces — an automated multi-horizon instruction annotator, the dataset (**100 expert players, temporally aligned actions and multi-horizon instructions**), and a reproducible offline+online benchmark where the **online track checks whether offline scores actually predict gameplay and localises failures to specific steps**. Results show a clear task-difficulty hierarchy and wide capability spread across VLMs, GUI agents, coding models and game agents. Public release intended as a "standardised yardstick". **The most rigorous public evaluation of agents in a game environment to date.** [arXiv:2609.25001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25001) · [GitHub](https://github.com/TencentARC/GameHorizon)
- ⭐ **"Is Personalized Modality Weighting Actually Personalized?" — a controlled audit of per-user weighting claims in multimodal recommenders.** Short answer from the title alone is the useful one, and it belongs on the reading list for anyone about to accept a vendor's personalisation claim at face value. From the 21 Sep RecSys-adjacent batch. [DailyArXiv](https://github.com/yuque01/DailyArXiv/issues/316)
- ⭐ **Spi-Fly: a fruit-fly-inspired spiking network learns odours in three shots with ~5 orders of magnitude less memory than backprop.** *Date caveat: the OIST work was published 24 Aug 2026; the 21 Sep write-up is a re-report, not a new release.* Still worth carrying because it is the clearest non-transformer result in the window. OIST with TU Eindhoven and Kiel: models the fly olfactory circuit's sparse coding (**~5% of Kenyon cells active per smell**), reaching peak performance on real gas-sensor data after **three exposures per odour** and one-shot on synthetic. **Avoids catastrophic forgetting when learning new categories**, runs at **4–6 bit weights**, aimed at compact hardware. [OIST](https://www.oist.jp/news-center/news/2026/8/24/how-decipher-smells-fruit-fly) · [TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-08-fruit-fly-algorithm-odors-samples.html)
- **IntBMoE tops the Hugging Face daily papers at 90 upvotes.** Block-level conditioning integrated into expert composition for **full-participation MoE** — the argument being that block-level signals let every expert usefully contribute instead of degrading to sparse top-k routing. Ships tf-idf expert analyses across masked layers as diagnostics. [HF paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.21346)
- ⭐ **Designer-RSI evolves agentic procedural memory from real user traffic.** Replays base and evolved agent variants on user-generated design tasks to keep improving memory of successful procedures **without labelled supervision**. Mechanically the same shape as learning a recommendation policy from production traffic. [HF paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.22086)

## ⭐ Flagged for Frank

1. **The open-weight frontier caught the paid frontier this weekend, and the number that matters is $2.62M.** MiMo-V2.6-Pro and Grok 4.7 both score 46 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — one is MIT-licensed with weights, code and training environment published, the other is a metered API. Xiaomi disclosed that Pro's RL run took **30 steps over ~750,000 trajectories in under six days for $2.62M, and Flash for $850k**. That is a capex number a large operator could contemplate, not a frontier-lab number, and it is the first time the cost of reaching the top of the open-weights table has been published with this specificity. The second-order implication for Bally's is about **vendor leverage rather than self-hosting**: the moment a genuinely open model at the same index score exists, the commercial case for any single model vendor's pricing and terms weakens, and model-portability becomes a realistic contract ask rather than a rhetorical one. Worth asking whoever owns the vendor relationships whether current agreements assume a single provider — because the alternative just became credible. Caveat before anyone quotes the benchmark internally: **Xiaomi's DeepSWE figures are on its own internal harness and have not been independently reproduced.**

2. **The agent client is now a bigger exposure than the agent model, and that's three weekends in a row.** Plugin4Shell (17 Sep, agent trusts a `git checkout` exit code), ZCode (18–21 Sep, agent silently uploads 42,411 workspace files including full `.git` history to overseas object storage), Muse for Mac (21 Sep, any local process steals the auth token and redirects dictated prompts to attacker servers). **None of these is a model failure. None would be caught by a guardrail, a monitor, or a prompt-injection filter.** They are ordinary application security failures in software that happens to have an LLM inside and, critically, broad filesystem and credential access by design. The practical consequence for the prototyping platform RFC is that AI tooling needs to sit inside normal third-party software review — client binaries, telemetry defaults, outbound network destinations — not in a separate "AI tools" lane that skips it. The ZCode case is the sharpest illustration: **the exfiltration was a default-on "codebase indexing" feature, not an attack.** Someone shipped it on purpose. Ask of any coding agent under evaluation: what does it send, where, and is it on by default.

3. **Shopify's default-on agentic checkout is the closest read-across to iGaming that has appeared all month.** A shopping agent can now complete a purchase through Shop Pay across every Shopify store with no merchant setup, using **verified buyer credentials scoped to a single transaction** so the agent never touches card data. That single-use-credential pattern is exactly the shape any agent-initiated transaction in a regulated gambling context would need — a permission that is narrow, bounded, and expires, rather than an agent holding standing account access. But the gambling version has constraints commerce doesn't: **deposit limits, self-exclusion registers, affordability checks and RG interventions all have to bind the agent, not the UI it bypasses.** If an agent can complete a deposit without rendering the responsible-gambling interstitial, the control has been architecturally removed even though nobody deleted it. That is the question to get ahead of, and the UCP spec is now a concrete artefact to test it against rather than a hypothetical. Note also the split: **Amazon blocked Muse and is litigating; Shopify made it default-on.** Operators will face that same fork.

4. **Two results this week say the same thing about where agent effort should go, and one says something uncomfortable about trusting a vendor's effort setting.** Harness-Zero distills harness behaviour into weights and then **beats the harness it learned from — 44.3% without it versus 41.7% with it**; RRSI shows regularised self-improvement generalises **while cutting policy tokens 30%**. Combined with last week's harness-design paper, the consistent finding is that elaborate harness engineering is a temporary scaffold that the model absorbs, so effort is better spent on task decomposition and evaluation than on prompt and tool scaffolding you will throw away. The uncomfortable companion finding is the Fable 5 analysis: **median delivered thinking fell ~41% below July in late August at the same advertised effort setting, with median invocation thinking hitting zero for about a week.** Mechanism unidentified, and the author says so — but the operational lesson holds regardless of cause. If an agent's behaviour is a dependency, **the amount of reasoning you actually receive is not a constant and is not something you control**, which means any evaluation baseline needs re-running on a schedule rather than being measured once at procurement and cited for a year. For RG or fraud tooling where a drop in reasoning depth degrades quietly rather than loudly, that's a monitoring requirement, not a curiosity.
