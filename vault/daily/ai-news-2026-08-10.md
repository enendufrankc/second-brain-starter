## Top Stories

- **EU AI Act enforcement is now live and biting** — the Commission's AI Office plus national authorities began enforcing the Act on Aug 2, with Article 50 transparency obligations now applying to providers and deployers. Noncompliance exposure: up to €15M or 3% of global turnover. [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714) · [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026)
- **Annex III high-risk deadline officially slipped to 2 Dec 2027** — stand-alone recruitment, credit scoring, education, law enforcement and critical-infrastructure systems no longer had to comply on Aug 2. AI embedded in Annex I products (medical devices, machinery, toys) moves to Aug 2028. Material breathing room for anyone building scoring/profiling systems in the EU. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/6/what-came-into-force-with-the-eus-ai-act-this-week-and-what-didnt)
- **Anthropic is standing up an in-house AI chip design team** — plans to co-design hardware and models for faster, cheaper inference. A real strategic shift for a company that has so far been a pure compute buyer. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/anthropic-is-hiring-an-ai-chip-design-team/)
- **DeepMind leadership fallout continues** — Hassabis now Chairman/Alphabet Chief Scientist, Koray Kavukcuoglu running DeepMind as SVP; Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le gone to found Discovery Loop. GOOG dropped 4% on the news and Gemini 3.5 Pro remains delayed. [Memeburn](https://memeburn.com/google-deepmind-brain-drain-2026/)

Deal *volume* barely moved; it's all mega-rounds. [Crescendo](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-vc-investment-deals-in-ai-startups)

## Research & Papers

- **Karpathy's `autoresearch`** keeps circulating — an open-source greedy experiment loop where a coding agent ran ~700 experiments over two days on nanochat training and found 20 real optimizations. The clearest existing template for "agent does the science, human sets the loop." [GitHub](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/17/andrej-karpathy-loop-autonomous-ai-agents-future/)
- **Karpathy is now at Anthropic** on the pretraining team — worth noting for where the scaling-vs-architecture debate lands next. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrej_Karpathy)

Useful if you ever need external benchmarks for agent harness quality rather than raw model quality. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07462) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.08590)

## Relevant to Frank's Work 🎯

- **The Annex III delay to Dec 2027 is the single most actionable item today.** If any Bally's recommendation, scoring, or player-profiling system was being scoped against an Aug 2026 high-risk deadline, that pressure just moved out ~16 months. Article 50 transparency obligations *did* land though — AI-generated content labelling and disclosure to users are live now, and the AI Office published a voluntary Code of Practice with standard icons you could adopt. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/6/what-came-into-force-with-the-eus-ai-act-this-week-and-what-didnt) · [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714)

That's a concrete pattern to benchmark our own approach against. [Smartico](https://www.smartico.ai/blog-post/predictive-game-recommendations-ai-curating-personal-igaming-journeys) · [Yogonet](https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2025/09/26/115546-ai-personalization-in-igaming-2026-smart-odds-hypertailored-ux-and-player-safety)
