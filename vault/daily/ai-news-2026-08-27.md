## ⭐ Flagged for Frank

**iGaming / recommendation systems**

- The sector narrative for 2026 has shifted from "recommendation engine" to **real-time decisioning** — content curation, bonus optimisation, and predictive intervention on the same behavioural signal stream. Same architecture serving monetisation and safeguarding. [gr8.tech](https://gr8.tech/blog/ai-in-igaming-a-look-into-machine-learning-and-personalized-gaming-experiences/)
- **Responsible Gambling 2.0** — proactive detection of escalating bet size / session length, with soft interventions (pauses, cooling-off). This is the defensible framing for personalisation investment under AI Act scrutiny. [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/06/09/how-ai-quietly-restructured-online-casino-personalization-and-problem-gambling-detection/)

- Market: $110.8B (2025) → $130.5B (2026), 17.8% CAGR, $249B projected by 2030. [Symphony Solutions](https://symphony-solutions.com/insights/igaming-technology-trends-2026)

**Agentic AI**

- Three credible enterprise agent platforms landed in August (Bedrock AgentCore GA, Agent Lightning, Gemini Enterprise). The build-vs-buy question for internal agent infrastructure is now genuinely open — worth a look before the next platform RFC.
- Claude shared memory across chat and Cowork changes what a persistent-context assistant can do without custom state plumbing.

**Compliance**

- Article 50 is live *now*. Any AI-generated player communication or synthetic content in EU markets needs disclosure and marking. Anthropic's global watermarking means Claude-generated output already carries a mark — relevant to how you'd evidence compliance.
