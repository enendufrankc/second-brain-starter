## Research & Papers

- **Karpathy's AutoResearch is the template worth internalising** — an autonomous ML experimentation loop that reads training code, proposes changes, runs jobs, measures, then commits or rolls back. 66K+ GitHub stars within a month of its March release. 🎯 The commit-or-rollback-on-measured-result pattern generalises well beyond ML training; it's the right shape for automated experimentation on recommendation models. [Verdent guide](https://www.verdent.ai/guides/what-is-autoresearch-karpathy) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrej_Karpathy)
- **Karpathy's "Software 3.0" framing** — software programmed through prompts, context, agents, tools, memory and verification rather than code. Useful vocabulary for explaining agentic architecture to non-engineers. [Sequoia Ascent notes](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)
- **The LeCun/Altman split is now a real fork in the field** — scale-is-everything vs. scale-is-a-dead-end, with incompatible research programmes behind each. Worth tracking rather than picking a side; it determines whether to architect around frontier-model dependence or around smaller specialised models. [Medium: The Week AI Split in Two](https://medium.com/atlas-anywhere/the-week-ai-split-in-two-7097cb401c69)
- **Agent framework consolidation is done** — every major lab now ships one: OpenAI Agents SDK, Google ADK, Anthropic Agent SDK, Microsoft Semantic Kernel/AutoGen, HuggingFace Smolagents. Google's ADK is the code-first option covering tools, sessions, memory, evals, multi-agent patterns and deployment. Framework choice is now a portability question, not a capability one. [KDnuggets](https://www.kdnuggets.com/10-agentic-ai-frameworks-you-should-know-in-2026)
