## ⭐ Flagged for Frank

1. **Haiku 5.5 and Strands Box together change the cost of agent monitoring.** A capable small model at $0.10/M input, $0.01/M cache reads and adjustable effort makes an LLM-based reviewer on every tool call affordable, and Strands Box shows the AWS architecture: deterministic, history-aware rules outside the agent, with a model only where judgement is needed. **Second-order: the winning design is two layers, cheap deterministic rate and sequence rules first (what Dogwood does), then a small-model check only for ambiguous calls, which keeps both cost and the latency tail low; and because Decisions API, Haiku and clef all sit near the same price, you can make the judge swappable and avoid dependence on any one vendor.** Caveat: Box is macOS-only today and Haiku's numbers are vendor-stated, so benchmark on your own call traces.

2. **MAS is the first detailed regulator template for AI in a licensed industry, and it maps closely onto gambling.** Independent pre-deployment review, a live AI inventory including undisclosed third-party AI, drift monitoring and fallback plans are exactly what an MGA or UKGC auditor could ask next. **Second-order: "independent review" means the team that builds a recommender or agent cannot be the team that signs it off, so decide now who the independent party is and keep the evidence (inventory, test suites, decision logs) in a form that can be shown; vendors that cannot disclose where they use AI will become a liability.** In force date is 7 Oct 2027, so there is time to build it in rather than retrofit.

3. **The Copilot CLI "zombie instruction" finding, the Pwn2Own LiteLLM and Codex results, and ColPali inversion all say the agent's inputs and its stored data are attack surface.** Hidden page instructions steer agents; gateways are exploited; stored retrieval vectors can be inverted back to the document (98.4%). **Second-order: for personalisation, embedding stores of player behaviour or content should be treated as personal data (access control, retention, pooling or noise as a mitigation), and any agent that reads untrusted content should run without secrets in reach and with egress allow-listed, which is what Strands Box-style isolation provides.** This also strengthens the case for on-device embeddings (EmbeddingGemma 2) since vectors then never leave the player's device.

4. **OpenAI's maths dispute is a reminder to demand verifiable artefacts, not claims.** Lean certificates are the useful part of Aaronson's report; the 700+ manuscripts and withheld prompts are the weak part. **Second-order: the same standard applies to any agent claim you evaluate for a hackathon or production (a replayable trace, the exact prompt, per-task compute), and a small-model decision gate such as Haiku 5.5 is a sensible way to build that audit trail cheaply.** Treat all OpenAI figures here as unverified.
