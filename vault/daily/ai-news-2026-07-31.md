## 🎯 Frank-Relevant Flags Summary

**Act on this week:**

- **EU AI Act Article 50 lands Monday, August 2.** Chatbot disclosure and synthetic-content marking are enforceable. Any player-facing AI surface — recommendation copy, chat assistants, generated promo creative — needs a disclosure story. Pair with the Act's risk-based safeguards on harmful-behaviour detection already flagged for iGaming.
- **MCP spec 2026-07-28 is stateless at the transport layer.** This is a breaking-shaped change. Anything built against the older stateful assumption should be checked before further agent work.

**Watch:**

- **Agent security has numbers now.** Opus 5 Auto Mode reporting 0% prompt-injection success across 129 browser scenarios is the first credible benchmark to cite in internal agent-safety conversations. Counterweight: Anthropic's own eval-containment failure and OpenAI's Hugging Face breach both landed this fortnight — containment, not capability, is the live risk.
- **iGaming recommendation competition.** ZingBrain/Vegangster follows BetConstruct's game-recommendation push last week. Two personalisation-engine moves in five days; the vendor layer is getting crowded.
