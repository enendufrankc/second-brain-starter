## Personal Rules

### Financial Rules (11 Non-Negotiables — set 25 Apr 2026)

> Source: [finance/index.md](../finance/index.md) | [Bank statement deep dive](../finance/sessions/2026-04-25-bank-statement-analysis.md)

1. **Never invest while carrying consumer debt above 20% APR.** Aqua is 44.9%. Kill it first.
2. **Maintain 3-month emergency fund (£7,725) minimum.** Pause investments if it drops below this.
3. **Never release money to partners before legal structures are confirmed.** Chisom needs CAC registration first.
4. **Track every Ebuka return in writing.** Pause scaling if returns stop for 3+ months.
5. **No new ventures until at least 2 existing ones generate revenue.** Ideas are parked.
6. **Life insurance must stay active while family depends on one salary.** Get this done immediately.
7. **LISA money is for the house — never withdraw early (25% penalty).**
8. **NO Klarna, NO buy-now-pay-later, NO eBay shopping. Debit card and cash only.**
9. **Nnenna gets £600/month fixed allowance. No ad hoc top-ups.** One transfer on payday.
10. **No loans to friends or family while consumer debt exists or emergency fund is below £7,725.** Clinton already owes ~£890.
11. **Monthly bank statement review on the 1st — no exceptions.** Export all 3 accounts, check vs budget.

### Spending Discipline (Stabilisation Phase — May–Oct 2026)

- **Shopping ban:** No eBay, OnBuy, Klarna, or non-essential online purchases. If it's not food, transport, or bills — wait 48 hours, then still probably don't buy it.
- **Eating out cap:** £30/month maximum. That's 2 meals out. Cook at home.
- **Transport budget:** £100/month. Bus OR car per trip, not both. Stop the double-spending.
- **No barber:** Clipper bought, cut at home. Saves £360+/year.
- **Subscriptions:** Only Anthropic (£6), Railway x1 (£3.75), Cloudflare (£8). Everything else cancelled or under review.
- **Klarna app deleted.** If you reinstall it, you've broken your own rules.
- **No lending money.** Not to Clinton, not to anyone. Not until debts are cleared and emergency fund is full.

### Daily Money Habits

- Check bank balance before spending anything discretionary
- Use debit card or cash only — no credit, no BNPL
- Ask before every purchase: "Is this food, transport, or a bill? If not, don't buy it."
- Log any unexpected expenses in the finance tracker

### Monthly Money Habits (1st of each month)

- Export Lloyds, Revolut, and HSBC statements
- Drop into `finance/transactions/YYYY-MM/` folder
- Check: Nnenna allowance stayed at £600? Any top-ups?
- Check: Any Klarna/BNPL purchases? (answer must be NO)
- Check: Variable spending under £440?
- Check: Aqua payment went through at £700?
- Check: Emergency fund balance in HSBC?
- Check: All cancelled subscriptions still cancelled?
- Update finance tracker with new balances
- Log any deviations and reasons
