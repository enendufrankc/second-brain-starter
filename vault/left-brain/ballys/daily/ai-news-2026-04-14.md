# AI News Briefing — April 14, 2026

([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/)) ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openais-new-100-chatgpt-pro-plan-targets-claude-max-with-five-times-the-codex-access))

([Visual Studio Magazine](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/articles/2026/04/06/microsoft-ships-production-ready-agent-framework-1-0-for-net-and-python.aspx))

5. **Anthropic revenue run rate hits $30B** — Up from ~$9B at end of 2025, a roughly 3x gain in about 90 days. The company also launched Claude for Word, completing full Office suite integration. ([Augment](https://augment.market/pulse/darios-notes-on-sam-anthropics-30b-run-rate)) ([OpenPR](https://www.openpr.com/news/4468672/anthropic-rolls-out-claude-for-word-add-in-now-full-microsoft))
