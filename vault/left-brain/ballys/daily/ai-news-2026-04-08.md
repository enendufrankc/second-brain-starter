## AI Voices — What They're Saying

**Andrej Karpathy** shared his "LLM Wiki" concept — a system where you point an LLM at raw research materials and it builds a complete, interlinked wiki from scratch. His personal research wiki has grown to ~100 articles and 400K words. He also demoed "Dobby," an OpenClaw agent that discovered and controlled smart home devices via reverse-engineered APIs. ([Analytics Vidhya](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2026/04/llm-wiki-by-andrej-karpathy/))

**Sam Altman** released a 13-page policy blueprint comparing the coming AI transformation to the Progressive Era and New Deal. He warned that AI-enabled cyberattacks and bioweapons are no longer theoretical risks. Meanwhile, a lengthy New Yorker profile by Ronan Farrow added to scrutiny of his leadership. ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5817906-openai-ai-policy-recommendations/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/07/openai-drama-sam-altman-ipo-anthropic-cybersecurity-risks-eye-on-ai/))

**Dario Amodei** remains in the spotlight after his leaked internal memo blasting the Trump administration and OpenAI. He's since apologized, but shareholder concerns about his volatility are growing. Anthropic's unique Slack "notebook" culture — where employees publicly challenge the CEO — was profiled. ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/04/51653555/just-argue-with-dario-inside-anthropics-ai-culture-where-employees-publicly-challenge-ceo-on-slack))

**Yann LeCun** is building AMI Labs with $1B+ in funding, betting that "world models" — not language models — are the path to real AI. He left Meta after 12 years when Zuckerberg put 28-year-old Alexandr Wang in charge of AI strategy. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/09/yann-lecuns-ami-labs-raises-1-03-billion-to-build-world-models/))

**Jim Fan** (NVIDIA) continues pushing the "Second Pre-training Paradigm" — world models as the foundation for physical AI. His team's DreamZero project demonstrates robot-to-robot and human-to-robot transfer learning using video as a universal bridge. Everything is open-sourced. ([Jim Fan](https://jimfan.me/))

**Simon Willison** described November 2025 as the coding agent inflection point where agents crossed from "mostly works" to "almost always does what you told it to do." He warns about the emerging "dark factory" pattern where nobody writes or reviews code, and notes that prompt injection remains an unsolved threat. He says ~95% of his code is now AI-generated. ([Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/5/building-with-ai/) · [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/an-ai-state-of-the-union))
