# AI News Briefing — April 13, 2026

## Top Stories

1. **Anthropic's Claude Mythos deemed too dangerous for public release** — Anthropic introduced Claude Mythos Preview, a model with advanced cybersecurity capabilities that can autonomously detect and exploit software vulnerabilities. Rather than releasing it publicly, Anthropic restricted access to ~40 organizations through an invitation-only initiative called [Project Glasswing](https://red.anthropic.com/2026/mythos-preview/). U.S. Treasury and Fed officials held [emergency meetings with bank CEOs](https://nationaltoday.com/us/dc/washington/news/2026/04/12/anthropics-claude-mythos-ai-sparks-emergency-us-banking-meetings/) over the systemic cybersecurity implications.

2. **Meta launches Muse Spark, breaks from open-source roots** — Meta debuted [Muse Spark](https://venturebeat.com/technology/goodbye-llama-meta-launches-new-proprietary-ai-model-muse-spark-first-since), its first proprietary AI model from Meta Superintelligence Labs led by Alexandr Wang. This marks a major departure from the open-source Llama series. On benchmarks, Muse Spark ranks fourth globally behind Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.4, and Claude Opus 4.6.

3. **Sam Altman's home attacked after OpenAI's "New Deal" policy proposal** — Someone [threw a Molotov cocktail at Altman's San Francisco home](https://sfstandard.com/2026/04/12/openai-wants-new-deal-ai-attack-sam-altman-s-home-made-urgent/) days after OpenAI published a policy blueprint calling for a public wealth fund, robot tax, and four-day workweeks. A suspect was arrested. Altman published a [personal blog post](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/11/sam-altman-responds-to-incendiary-new-yorker-article-after-attack-on-his-home/) responding to the attack and a New Yorker profile.

4. **MCP crosses 97 million installs, enters enterprise phase** — Anthropic's Model Context Protocol has hit 97M monthly SDK downloads and is now adopted by every major AI provider. The [2026 MCP Roadmap](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-mcp-roadmap/) focuses on enterprise pain points: audit trails, SSO-integrated auth, gateway behavior, and configuration portability.

5. **Microsoft ships Agent Framework 1.0** — Microsoft released its [production-ready Agent Framework 1.0](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/agent-framework/microsoft-agent-framework-version-1-0/) for .NET and Python, combining Semantic Kernel and AutoGen into a single open-source framework with full MCP support and A2A 1.0 arriving soon.
