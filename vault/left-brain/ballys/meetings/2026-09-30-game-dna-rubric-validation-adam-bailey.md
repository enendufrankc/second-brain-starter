## Summary
Frank walked Adam through the Game Experience (Game DNA) rubrics: tempo, looks and sound, mechanics, reward and meta game, each with three sub-dimensions. Adam gave domain input on spin cycle, anticipation, feedback timing and compliance wording. Frank also explained the recording criteria and how outputs will be consumed.

## Key Decisions
- Spin cycle is UKGC-regulated (min 2.5s); tempo should capture spin rhythm differences (fast spin + wait vs. full 2.5s), not just speed.
- Avoid labelling players as liking "fast-paced" games (Gambling Commission risk); prefer neutral naming like "similar tempo" and check with compliance.
- Metadata never shows the numeric breakdown, only tags.
- Each use case (Adaptive Layouts, SEO/marketing) runs its own A/B test.
- Recording criteria: open paytable, lowest bet, ~40 spins (~5 min), at least one win and bonus; re-record when games update.
