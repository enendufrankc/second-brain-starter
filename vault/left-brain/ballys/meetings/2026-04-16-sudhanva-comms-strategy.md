# Comms Strategy - Drudgery Survey

Sudhanva created a "What's Eating Your Time?" drudgery survey and needed help with messaging to get wider engagement without it feeling like job automation surveillance. Frank provided a 4-pillar comms strategy and they discussed on a 25-minute call.

- Consider "First 20 responses get their pain point fast-tracked for an AI prototype"
