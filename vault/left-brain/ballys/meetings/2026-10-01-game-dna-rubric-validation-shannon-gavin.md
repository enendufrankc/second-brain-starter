## Key Decisions
- Add anticipation (reel slow-down before bonus) to tempo; pair with near-miss/bonus frequency ratio.
- Add readability (features, symbols, unusual pay mechanic) and an intensity flag for safer-gambling review when anticipation, urgency, retention and tempo are high but readability is low.
- Do not score bonus payout from video (too volatile); score visuals/anticipation instead.
- Increase minimum spins per recording from 40 to 80 (players average 80-100 spins).
- Capture win celebration vs stake rule (no celebration for wins below stake) and record some spins after a feature.
- Treat "suspense" as a cross-cutting factor within existing dimensions, not necessarily a new dimension.
