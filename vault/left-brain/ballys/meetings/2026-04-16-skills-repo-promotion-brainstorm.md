# Skills Repo Promotion Plan - Brainstorm

## Summary
Brainstorm session for promoting the Ballys Skills Repo across the 60+ engineer Claude Code pilot. Discussed distribution channels, messaging strategy, and Apollo's complementary role.

## Key Decisions
- Two distribution channels agreed: (1) broadcast email to all tech, (2) meeting for Engineering Leads to showcase
- Al wants people who engage the most to advertise their skills (AI Champions channel approach)
- Apollo is complementary (distribution) not competing (curation) — no conflict
- Frank's messaging approach: "What would you automate if you could?" rather than "Tell us your repetitive tasks"
